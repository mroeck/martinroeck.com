## Current

- [**GBDI China and India Cooperation Incubator**](https://www.gbdi.io) (Global Industry Hub, 2026–2027). Extends the global building data work into the two markets that will add the most floor area this century. EPFL, <span class="org">RISE</span>. Principal Investigator.
- [**Global Whole Life Carbon Tracker for the Built Environment**](https://bidstats.uk/tenders/2025/W08/840957961) (UK Department for Energy Security and Net Zero, 2025–2026). Builds a global tracker of whole life carbon in construction, so progress can be measured against something. UCL, BPIE, <span class="org">RISE</span>, Arup. Work package lead, global building materials and life cycle emissions.
- [**INDICATE LIFE — National Building LCA Data and Policy Accelerator**](https://www.indicatedata.com/) (EU LIFE Clean Energy Transition, 2024–2027). Helps national consortia generate the building LCA data their governments need to set whole life carbon benchmarks and targets. Smith Innovation, KU Leuven, <span class="org">RISE</span>, BPIE, WorldGBC, with national consortia in Austria, Hungary, Italy and Luxembourg. Team lead and work package lead.
- [**Collaborative Carbon — Whole Life Carbon Assessments of EU Collaborative Housing**](http://www.collaborative-carbon.com) (Laudes Foundation, 2024–2026). Measures the whole life carbon of community-led housing across Europe, and what sufficiency strategies actually save. <span class="org">RISE</span> with einszueins and TU Delft. Principal Investigator.
- **Global Status Report for Buildings and Construction** (UNEP / GlobalABC, 2024–). The annual global stocktake of the sector's emissions and progress. <span class="org">GBDI</span>. Lead Advisor, Embodied Carbon.

## Concluded

- [**GBDI Global Building Data Initiative - Pilot Phase**](https://www.gbdi.io) (ClimateWorks Foundation, 2024–2025). Launched an open global data commons on resource use and upfront embodied carbon of new buildings. EPFL, <span class="org">RISE</span>. Principal Investigator.
- [**Analysis of Life Cycle Greenhouse Gas Emissions and Removals of EU Buildings and Construction**](https://c.ramboll.com/life-cycle-emissions-of-eu-building-and-construction) (European Commission DG GROW, 2022–2025). Quantified the baseline and the plausible futures for emissions and removals across the EU building stock to 2050. Ramboll, <span class="org">KU Leuven</span>, BPIE, TU Graz, IIASA, AAU BUILD, Politecnico di Milano. Team lead and task lead, baseline analysis and scenario modelling.
- [**INDICATE — National Building LCA Data Accelerator (pilot)**](https://www.indicatedata.com/) (Laudes Foundation, 2022–2024). Piloted the data accelerator in three countries, and showed the approach transfers. Smith Innovation, BPIE, <span class="org">KU Leuven</span>, WorldGBC, with national consortia in Ireland, Spain and Czechia. Team lead and work package lead, whole life carbon data and benchmarks.
- [**Supporting the Development of a Roadmap for the Reduction of Whole Life Carbon of Buildings**](https://c.ramboll.com/whole-life-carbon-reduction) (European Commission DG ENV, 2021–2024). Provided the archetype modelling behind the EU's roadmap for bringing down whole life carbon. Ramboll, BPIE, <span class="org">KU Leuven</span>. Team lead and task lead, whole life carbon archetype modelling.
- [**Towards Embodied Carbon Benchmarks for the European Building Industry**](https://c.ramboll.com/lets-reduce-embodied-carbon) (Laudes Foundation, 2021–2022). Established a first European baseline for embodied carbon from real building data, and a budget-based way to set targets against it. Ramboll, AAU BUILD, <span class="org">Martin Röck</span>. Task lead, European baseline for embodied carbon of buildings.
- [**IEA EBC Annex 72 — Assessing Life Cycle Related Environmental Impacts of Buildings**](https://annex72.iea-ebc.org/) (International Energy Agency and Austrian Federal Ministry, 2016–2023). Harmonised building LCA methods, workflows and tools across 27 countries. <span class="org">TU Graz</span> within an international consortium. Subtask co-lead, building assessment workflows and tools.
- [**PEF4buildings — Application of the Product Environmental Footprint Method to a New Office Building**](https://ec.europa.eu/environment/eussd/smgp/index.htm) (European Commission DG ENV, 2017–2018). Tested whether the EU's Product Environmental Footprint method works when applied to a whole building. VITO, KU Leuven, <span class="org">TU Graz</span>. LCA and PEF modeller.
